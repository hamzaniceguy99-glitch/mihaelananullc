Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will USCIS accept this?

We provide the certification USCIS requires, prepared properly. No translator can promise an agency's decision, and anyone who does is overstating what a certificate is.

What makes a translation certified?

A complete, literal translation plus a signed certificate identifying the translator and the document, attesting to accuracy. It is not notarisation, and it does not verify that the original is genuine.

Can you tell me what my document means for my case?

No. That is legal advice and belongs with an attorney. We will translate exactly what it says, which is the thing your attorney needs.

Do you charge per page?

No, on word count and complexity. Per-page pricing charges you for white space and penalises dense documents.

Will the interpreter help me answer?

No, and that is the point. An interpreter conveys what is said in both directions without adding or advising. Impartiality is what makes the conversation trustworthy.

Can you fix a machine translation?

Sometimes, and sometimes retranslating is cheaper. Fluent output with a reversed negative is harder to repair than to redo, and we will tell you which it is.
