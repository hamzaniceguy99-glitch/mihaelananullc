Program 02 · With a certificate of accuracy

Certified translations

A certified translation is complete and literal, accompanied by a signed certificate identifying the translator and the document. It certifies that the translation is accurate — it does not verify that the original is genuine, and no translator can promise an agency will accept a filing.

  • Per document
  • Individuals and firms
  • Quoted per document

Program 03 · Impartial, and complete

Interpreting

An interpreter conveys everything that is said, in both directions, without adding, omitting or advising. That includes not answering on your behalf when it would be quicker. Impartiality is the whole value of the role, and it is what makes an interpreted conversation trustworthy to both sides.

  • Hourly or half day
  • Individuals and firms
  • Quoted per booking

Program 04 · Including machine output

Proofreading & review

Machine translation is often nearly right, which is the dangerous kind of wrong — fluent text with a reversed negative or a term of art rendered literally. We review against the source and tell you honestly when a text needs retranslating rather than patching, because patching bad output sometimes costs more.
